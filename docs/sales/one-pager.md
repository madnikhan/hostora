# Hostora one-pager (sales)

**Hostora** — Run the floor. From booking to last pour.

**What it is:** Hospitality ops OS that **replaces patchwork** till + kitchen tickets + booking widgets. Software with optional installed hardware (US, UK & Europe).

**How we sell:**
- **Restaurant pack** · **Takeaway pack** · **Events pack** — fixed spine, go-live in days after a short floor survey (not a bespoke rewrite)

- **Food carts** — configured for mobile till, tickets, tight footprint

**Modules:** POS · Kitchen display · Guest QR & seating · Payments & sales reports · Analytics · Inventory · Staff / HR / attendance · Accounting (HMRC-ready) · Supervisor monitoring
